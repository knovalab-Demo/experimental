The parts that make it yours.

Four upgrades that change how a car looks, feels and drives, and an honest account of what each one costs. Ranges are indicative and vary by car and brand.

Part 1 of 4

Forged wheels & tyres

Why it matters

The only part of the car that touches the road. Wheel weight changes how quickly the car accelerates, brakes and reacts over bumps, and tyre grip sets the limit of everything else.

Why it costs what it does

Forged alloys are machined from a single block under heavy pressure, which is slow and wasteful. Good tyres use specialised rubber compounds that wear faster the more grip they give.

Indicative range₹1.2 – 4 lakh per set
Part 2 of 4

Brake discs & calipers

Why it matters

Stopping takes far more energy than going. Larger drilled discs and multi-piston calipers shed heat better, so your last stop of a fast drive feels like your first.

Why it costs what it does

Discs are precision-cast or carbon-ceramic, and calipers are machined and tested for heat. Fitting also needs matching pads, fluid and often new wheels to clear them.

Indicative range₹80,000 – 3 lakh per axle
Part 3 of 4

Splitter, skirts & rear wing

Why it matters

At speed, air lifts a car. Aero parts push it down, so the tyres press harder on the road and the car stays steady in corners and on fast highways.

Why it costs what it does

Carbon fibre is laid up by hand and baked in an autoclave. Shape matters more than looks, so good parts are designed, tested and fitted precisely.

Indicative range₹30,000 – 2 lakh
Part 4 of 4

Coilover suspension

Why it matters

Springs and dampers decide how the car holds the road and how it rides. Adjustable coilovers let us set ride height and stiffness for your roads and your driving.

Why it costs what it does

Quality dampers are built from many precisely machined parts and tuned on test rigs. Cheap sets ride badly and wear out fast, so this is where good value really matters.

Indicative range₹60,000 – 2.5 lakh
